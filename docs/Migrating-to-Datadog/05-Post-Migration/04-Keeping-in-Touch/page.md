## How to share these resources with your team

* Create a shared onboarding checklist that links to the Learning Resources and Architecture Center.
* Add a “Datadog updates” channel in your team chat for curated release notes, blog posts, and relevant webinars.
* Schedule regular brown-bag sessions to review new features and platform changes highlighted in release notes or the blog.
* Maintain a single source of truth (wiki or internal docs) that aggregates links, runbooks, and recommended learning paths.

## Quick checklist for staying current

* Subscribe to release notes and blog RSS.
* Follow Datadog on social media (LinkedIn, Twitter).
* Enroll teammates in guided learning paths or hands-on labs from Learning Resources.
* Monitor status and documentation pages for operational alerts and API changes.
* Participate in community forums for practical tips and troubleshooting patterns.
